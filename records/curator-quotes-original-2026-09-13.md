## Untouched — for the record

**yulali** — Yulali fills the gap between loud designer florals and the cold, cerebral naturals. It is warm, generous and deeply beautiful — the kind of fragrance you keep as a personal secret for as long as you can.

**meydan** — The Spirit of Dubai operates at a level of ambition few houses match. Meydan is their love letter to their city — powerful, polished and unforgettable. If you want to understand what Arabic perfumery can achieve at its highest, this is where you start.

**rose-01** — The fragrance we recommend to anyone who thinks they don't like rose perfumes. Rose-01 is proof that the rose is the most versatile raw material in perfumery — when handled with this much intelligence.

**asad** — Asad is what we recommend to anyone who wants to smell effortlessly polished without effort. Office-appropriate, all-season capable, with the kind of quiet sillage that earns compliments without demanding them. At this price point, it is one of the most intelligent purchases in Arabic perfumery.

**royal-bleu** — Royal Bleu solves the eternal problem of the fresh-woody fragrance: how to be approachable without being forgettable. The ambergris dry-down elevates it above every designer alternative in its price class.

**melodie** — Melodie de Parfum does what great perfumery does: it tells a story. From the raspberry-saffron opening to the praline-vanilla finish, every stage is intentional and beautifully realised. A fragrance worth taking time with.

**turath** — If you want to understand why Arabic perfumery has captivated the world, Turath is the answer. It is the most historically rooted fragrance in our collection — a living connection to a 3,000-year tradition of scent.
